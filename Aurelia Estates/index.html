About Aurelia

We built the agency we wished existed.

Aurelia Estates started in 2011 with a simple frustration: buying a home felt more stressful than it needed to be. So we built a studio-sized agency around clarity — transparent pricing, honest inspections, and agents who answer their phones.

Today we operate across 14 cities, but the standard hasn't changed: we only list homes we'd be comfortable recommending to our own families.
